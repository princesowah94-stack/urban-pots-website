## Design Direction

All pages should feel:
- Modern and premium
- Clean and easy to navigate
- Minimal without feeling empty
- Designed to appeal to middle- to upper-end residential and commercial clients
- Soft and refined, with a luxury feel

**Avoid:** tacky branding, overly bold graphics, or loud fonts. Typography and overall design should feel architectural and developer-focused.

**Colours:** Restrained and sophisticated — neutral palette with minimal accent colours. Premium, timeless, and understated.

**Goal:** Position Urban Pots as a premium supplier and manufacturer trusted by developers, architects, landscapers, and discerning homeowners.

---

## Social Proof

Include:
- Google Reviews
- Project photos
- "Trusted by leading builders, landscapers, developers, and architects"
- Client logos and brands genuinely worked with

> Only display genuine clients and partners. Authentic social proof builds far more trust than anything manufactured.
