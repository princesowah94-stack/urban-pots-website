## How It Works

Create a visual "How It Works" section with images and icons.

**Step 1: Request a FREE Quote**
Send through drawings, plans, dimensions, quantities and preferred finish. Quotes are typically provided within 1–3 business days depending on project scope.

**Step 2: Confirm Project Details**
Review and approve the proposal, then secure the order with a deposit. Finalise planter dimensions, material selection and finish requirements.

**Step 3: Production & Lead Time**
Team begins manufacturing planter boxes in the Sydney yard. Lead times generally range between 4–16 weeks depending on project scope, material availability and production requirements.

**Step 4: Delivery or Collection**
Once completed, planter boxes are quality checked, securely wrapped, palletised and prepared for either collection or delivery.

## What Goes Into Our Planter Boxes

Include a section outlining the manufacturing process:
- Fibreglass, Fibre Cement or GRC construction
- Waterproofing system and primer coating
- Three coats of Dulux paint (standard or custom colour finish)
- Cut to size, glued and riveted for long-lasting structural support
- Integrated drainage holes
- Thick reinforced edging for additional strength
- Internal structural reinforcement on planter boxes exceeding 2 metres in length
