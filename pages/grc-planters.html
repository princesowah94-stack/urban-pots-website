What is GRC?

Glass Reinforced Concrete is a composite material combining Portland cement, fine aggregates, water, and alkali-resistant glass fibres. The result is a material that offers the aesthetic beauty of cast concrete with enhanced strength, durability, and design flexibility.

Aesthetic Control

Full control over surface finishes: smooth, board-formed, aggregate-exposed, or polished.

Design Freedom

Complex shapes and custom details impossible with other materials.

Exceptional Durability

40+ years in harsh outdoor conditions without degradation.

Lightweight

40% lighter than equivalent reinforced concrete — easier handling and installation.

GRC Frequently Asked Questions

What does GRC stand for and why should I care?

GRC stands for Glass Reinforced Concrete. It's a composite material that combines the aesthetic beauty of cast concrete with the durability and strength of glass fibres. The result is a material that can last 40+ years with minimal maintenance, making it ideal for premium residential and heritage projects where longevity is valued.

Is GRC suitable for outdoor installations?

Yes, absolutely. GRC is specifically designed for outdoor use. It has excellent resistance to:

  • Water penetration (<5% absorption)
  • UV degradation
  • Freeze-thaw cycles
  • Salt spray (coastal environments)
  • Acid rain and atmospheric pollution

GRC is rated Class A (highest durability class) under Australian standards.

How much maintenance does GRC require?

Minimal to none. Unlike other materials, GRC does not require regular sealing, painting, or cleaning. Simply rinse occasionally with water to remove dust and debris. GRC will age gracefully, developing a natural patina over time that many designers appreciate.

Can you create custom colours and finishes?

Yes. We offer full control over finish and colour:

  • Finishes: Smooth cast, board-formed, exposed aggregate, polished
  • Pigmentation: Custom colour matching from Dulux codes or your own samples
  • Aggregates: Bespoke stone and material selection for exposed aggregate finishes

Lead time may extend 2–4 weeks for highly customised finishes.

Is GRC environmentally sustainable?

Yes. GRC offers several sustainability benefits:

  • Low embodied energy: Uses less material than reinforced concrete
  • Long lifespan: 40+ years means no replacement waste
  • Recyclable: At end-of-life, GRC can be recycled or used as aggregate
  • No maintenance chemicals: Unlike other materials, GRC requires no sealers or paints

For LEED and green building certifications, GRC is an excellent choice.

What is the cost of GRC planters vs other materials?

GRC is typically more expensive than fibreglass or fibre cement due to its premium durability and customisation options. However, when considering total cost of ownership (installation, maintenance, replacement), GRC is often more economical:

  • No replacement costs for 40+ years
  • No maintenance, sealing, or re-painting
  • No performance degradation over time

Contact us for a detailed cost comparison on your specific project.

Can GRC be cast into custom shapes?

Yes. GRC can be cast into virtually any shape or size, limited only by logistics and mould fabrication. We've produced:

  • Complex geometric forms
  • Curved and organic shapes
  • Sculptural planters with custom detailing
  • Large-scale architectural installations (100+ units)

Complex custom shapes will extend lead time by 4–6 weeks.
