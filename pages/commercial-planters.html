Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about commercial projects and bulk orders.

What is the minimum order quantity?

We accommodate projects from 10 units upwards. Smaller quantities may have longer lead times. Contact us to discuss your project scope.

What is your typical lead time for commercial orders?

Lead times vary by material and customisation level:

  • Fibreglass: 6–8 weeks
  • Fibre Cement: 8–12 weeks
  • Thai Limestone (imported): 12–16 weeks

We can discuss fast-track options for tight timelines.

Can you provide samples before we commit?

Yes. We supply colour swatches, material samples, and mockup photos at no charge. For large orders, we can produce a single prototype unit for approval before full manufacturing begins.

What payment terms do you offer for bulk orders?

We offer flexible payment terms for commercial projects:

  • Deposit: 30% upon order confirmation
  • Progress payment: 40% at 50% manufacturing completion
  • Final payment: 30% upon delivery

Payment plans available for orders exceeding $100k.

Do you offer delivery and installation services?

We coordinate delivery to your site across Australia. Installation is your contractor's responsibility, but we provide detailed installation guides and on-site support by request (additional fee applies).

What warranty do you provide?

All commercial planters come with a 12-month structural warranty covering manufacturing defects. Material-specific durability guarantees:

  • Fibre Cement: 15-year durability in outdoor conditions
  • Fibreglass: 12-year durability in outdoor conditions
  • Thai Limestone: 25–40 year durability in outdoor conditions
Can you match existing Dulux colours or custom paint codes?

Absolutely. We work with Dulux colour codes and can custom-match paint colours from your architectural specification. Provide the colour reference at the briefing stage.
