Large Outdoor Pots — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers ask about big planters — sizing, materials, weight, durability and delivery.

How big can large outdoor pots be?

We make large outdoor pots from around 600mm up to 1.5 metres and beyond — in height, diameter or length. Because every pot is made to order, we can match an exact size for a courtyard, driveway entry, rooftop terrace or commercial landscape.

Which material is best for large pots outdoors?

For big pots that live outside in full sun, wind and rain, natural Thai limestone and fibreglass are the top choices. Thai limestone gives a solid, premium natural-stone look and lasts 25–40 years; fibreglass is far lighter — easier to move and to install on rooftops or balconies — and lasts 15+ years. Fibre cement sits in between.

Are large outdoor pots heavy? Can they go on a balcony or rooftop?

It depends on the material. Thai limestone is solid stone and best at ground level; fibreglass and lightweight composites weigh a fraction as much, which makes them ideal for balconies, rooftops and anywhere with weight limits. Tell us where the pots are going and we'll recommend the right build.

Will large outdoor pots crack or fade in the Australian sun?

Our outdoor pots are built for Australian conditions — UV-stable finishes that resist fading, and materials engineered to handle heat, frost and heavy rain without cracking. Thai limestone and fibreglass both carry multi-year outdoor durability.

Can I get a custom size, shape or colour?

Yes — large outdoor pots are our specialty and almost everything is made to order. Choose the height, diameter and shape (round, square, trough or tapered) and the finish, and we can colour-match to a Dulux code or your design spec.

How much do large outdoor pots cost?

Price depends on size, material and quantity. As a guide, fibreglass is the most budget-friendly, fibre cement is mid-range, and Thai limestone is the premium option. Send us your sizes and how many you need and we'll quote within a couple of business days.

Do you deliver large pots across Sydney and Australia?

Yes. We deliver large outdoor pots across the Sydney metro area and arrange freight Australia-wide. For big or bulk orders we coordinate delivery to your site — just let us know the access details.
