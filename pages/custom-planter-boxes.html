Frequently Asked

Answers to common questions about custom planter boxes, pricing, timeline, and materials.

What's the lead time? +

8-14 weeks from order confirmation to delivery, depending on material availability and manufacturing capacity. We'll confirm an exact date during consultation.

What materials are available? +

We manufacture in commercial-grade fibre cement and fibreglass. Both are waterproofed and primed, ready for your finishing touches.

Can I change my order after submitting? +

Yes. We'll confirm your specs during consultation before manufacturing begins. Changes after production starts may incur additional fees.

How is pricing calculated? +

Base price depends on material and finished size. Wall thickness adds $5 per 25mm increment. Custom RGB colours add $25 for sourcing and colour matching.

What's the warranty? +

All custom planters come with a 2-year manufacturing defect warranty. Waterproofing is guaranteed for 5 years under normal conditions.

Do you offer trade / bulk discounts? +

Yes. Landscapers, designers, and architects can access wholesale pricing. View our trade program.
