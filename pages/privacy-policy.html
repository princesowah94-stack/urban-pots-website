Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

1. Introduction

Urban Pots Australia ("we," "us," "our," or "Urban Pots") operates urbanpots.com.au and related online services (the "Website"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our Website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information in the following ways:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Contact forms: When you submit an enquiry, quote request, or contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, and message content.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect login credentials, billing and shipping address, and payment information.
  • Design configurations: When using our 3D planter configurator, we temporarily store your design preferences (dimensions, materials, colours) in your browser's local storage. These designs are sent to us only when you submit a quote request.
  • Communications: If you email us, call us, or contact us through social media, we store records of that communication.
  • Payment information: We do not directly store credit card details. Payment processing is handled by secure third-party providers (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser and device data: We collect information about your device, browser type, operating system, and unique identifiers.
  • Browsing activity: We collect information about pages you visit, how long you spend on them, and links you click, using Google Analytics and similar tracking tools.
  • IP address and location: We collect your IP address, which may reveal approximate geographic location (state/territory level in Australia).
  • Cookies and local storage: We use cookies to remember preferences, track sessions, and improve user experience. Design configurations are stored in browser localStorage.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill your orders, quotes, and service requests
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry, order status, or account
  • To send you promotional materials and newsletters (with your consent)
  • To improve Website functionality, user experience, and customer service
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service
  • To conduct market research and analytics
  • To personalise your experience on the Website

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We share information with contractors and service providers who assist us (e.g., payment processors, email service providers, hosting providers, analytics platforms). These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and only use your information for the purposes specified.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Business transfers: If Urban Pots is acquired, merged, or assets are transferred, your information may be part of that transaction. We will provide notice before your personal information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • Business partners: With your consent, we may share information with selected partners for joint marketing or services.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure socket layer (SSL) encryption for data in transit
  • Password protection and restricted access to personal information
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure storage of sensitive information

However, no method of transmission over the internet or storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the Website at your own risk.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Quote and order information is retained for 7 years for accounting and legal purposes
  • Contact form submissions are retained for 2 years for customer service and dispute resolution
  • Browser localStorage (design configurations) is stored only in your browser and cleared when you delete browser data
  • Website analytics data is typically retained for 26 months
  • Marketing communication preferences are retained until you unsubscribe

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time, subject to legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correct: You may request that we correct inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal information.
  • Right to delete: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to opt-out: You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in emails or contacting us.
  • Right to complain: If you have concerns about how we handle your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on the Website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and track your activity.

8.1 Types of Cookies

  • Essential cookies: Required for the Website to function (e.g., session management, security).
  • Performance cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the Website (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and personalise your experience.
  • Marketing cookies: Track your behaviour for advertising and remarketing purposes.

8.2 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, blocking cookies may affect Website functionality.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

Our Website may contain links to external websites and services (e.g., Google Maps, social media platforms, payment processors). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

The Website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by providing notice on the Website or via email. Your continued use of the Website following such notice constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

13. Australian Privacy Act Compliance

This Privacy Policy is compliant with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. For more information about your privacy rights in Australia, visit the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
