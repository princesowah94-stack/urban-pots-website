About Our Studio

Urban Pots Since 2015

Family-owned planter manufacturer in Western Sydney. Trusted by architects, developers, and landscapers across Australia.

Our Story

Urban Pots was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to design and manufacture high-quality planters that combine aesthetic beauty with functional durability. What started as a small workshop in Penrith has grown into a trusted supplier for residential, commercial, and landscape projects across Australia.

Every planter we create is handcrafted with attention to detail. We work directly with architects, developers, and landscapers to deliver custom solutions that exceed expectations. Our commitment to quality materials, precision manufacturing, and responsive customer service has made us a preferred partner for discerning designers and project managers.

Today, Urban Pots operates a state-of-the-art workshop in Western Sydney, employing skilled craftspeople who take pride in their work. We've supplied over 10,000+ planters to projects ranging from intimate residential gardens to landmark commercial developments.
