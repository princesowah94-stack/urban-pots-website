What Goes Into Our Cylinders

Commercial-grade manufacturing meets meticulous craftsmanship. Every cylinder built to outlast the competition.

✓

Fibreglass, Fibre Cement or Thai Limestone

Choose the material that fits your project: lightweight fibreglass, durable fibre cement, or premium natural Thai limestone for heritage and luxury installations.

✓

Waterproofing System and Primer Coating

Complete waterproofing and primer coat ensure long-lasting protection from Sydney's harsh weather.

✓

Three Coats of Dulux Paint

Standard or custom colour finish. Every cylinder gets three coats of premium Dulux paint for durability and aesthetic perfection.

✓

Precision Cut, Glued & Reinforced

Precision cut to size, professionally glued, and reinforced for long-lasting structural integrity under commercial loads.

✓

Integrated Drainage Holes

Strategic drainage placement prevents water pooling and extends cylinder lifespan.

✓

Thick Reinforced Edging

Heavy-duty edging resists damage from impact, weather, and installation handling.

✓

Large-Format Internal Reinforcement

Cylinders exceeding 1 metre in diameter include internal structural reinforcement for maximum stability.
