Quick Answers

Common questions about contacting us.

What's the fastest way to get a response?

Call us during business hours (Tue–Sat, 9am–4pm) on 0433 132 406. For general enquiries, email info@urbanpots.com.au and expect a response within 24 hours.

Can I visit the showroom without an appointment?

Our showroom is open Tuesday–Saturday, 9am–4pm. You're welcome to visit during these hours without an appointment. For Saturday visits or large group tours, please call ahead to confirm availability.

Do you take design consultations?

Yes. We offer free initial consultations for custom projects. Send us your brief via email or contact form, and our design team will follow up to discuss your vision and provide a quote.

How long does it take to get a quote?

For standard products, we provide a quote immediately. For custom planters, we typically send a detailed quote within 2–3 business days after receiving your project brief.

What information do you need for a custom quote?

Provide:

  • Dimensions (L × W × H or diameter × height)
  • Quantity required
  • Preferred material (fibre cement, fibreglass, or GRC)
  • Colour or finish preference
  • Project timeline
  • Any special requirements (custom shapes, details, etc.)
Do you offer design services?

Our team collaborates on design refinement once you brief us on your vision. For architectural design services, we can recommend local landscape architects and designers we work with regularly.
