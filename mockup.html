Three material
families

Each with its own character, finish, and application. All custom-sized, primed, and finished in our Western Sydney workshop. GRC available on special request for prestige projects — 3–4 month lead time applies.

Standard

Fibre Cement

Durable, lightweight, architecturally clean. The workhorse of commercial installations — balconies, lobbies, large-scale residential developments.

Premium

Fibreglass

Unlimited colour matching, flawless smooth finish, exceptional longevity. The preferred choice of architects and luxury residential clients.

On Request

GRC — Glass Reinforced Concrete

The prestige choice for heritage and hospitality projects. Combines concrete aesthetics with superior strength and design flexibility.
