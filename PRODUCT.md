# Urban Pots — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Premium custom planter box manufacturer and retailer. The website is a marketing + commerce site that sells direct to consumers and handles B2B commercial enquiries. The Custom Planter Box service is the primary revenue driver.

## Register
brand

## Users

**Primary:**
- Property developers and construction companies (bulk commercial orders, project spec compliance)
- Landscapers and builders (trade relationships, custom sizing)
- Architects and designers (material versatility, design collaboration)

**Secondary:**
- High-net-worth individuals (premium aesthetics, statement pieces for homes)

## Brand Tone
Less "pot shop", more "architectural planting solutions partner". Premium, considered, architecturally aware — like Aesop applied to planters. Authority through restraint, not noise.

Inspiration references: Lipman Construction (lipman.com.au), Spaceful (spaceful.com.au).

Anti-references: generic Shopify pot stores, cheap-looking e-commerce, loud sale-focused design.

## Strategic Principles
1. Custom manufacturing is the differentiator — lead with it always
2. Local (Western Sydney) manufacturing is a trust anchor — genuine, not performative
3. Commercial/developer market is higher-value than retail — design should speak to B2B buyers first
4. Photography carries the page — copy amplifies, doesn't substitute
5. Position as "architectural planting solutions partner" not a product catalogue

## Key Metrics
- Custom quote form completions
- Trade account enquiries
- Time on Custom Planters section

## Anti-patterns
- No generic pot shop vibes
- No loud discount/sale messaging
- No cluttered layouts
- No stock photography (use real project photography)
